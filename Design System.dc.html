AccessibilityAll text pairs pass AA on their surface. Targets 48 px minimum (hearts and "+" get invisible padding). Every animation respects Reduce motion; sound has its own switch and stays silent in silent mode.
Arabic, laterLayouts use start/end, never left/right. Directional icons (back, chevrons, the scooter's path) mirror. Baloo Bhaijaan 2 matches Fredoka's round shapes. Prices stay Latin digits + "DH".
Copy voiceTutoiement, short, gourmand: "Je me régale !", "Remplir mon assiette". No guilt, no fake urgency: countdowns only for real drops.
